How these two curves are computed

Best in the pool is the highest-DockQ structure among the k drawn, chosen with the answer key. It is an upper bound on what any selector could return, not an achievable number, and it is what oracle-best-of-N means throughout this literature. Delivered is the structure the model's own confidence ranks first, with no ground truth. The two curves share their weighting and differ only in what they sort by.

On the DockQ scale 0.23 is the community's acceptable bar, 0.49 medium and 0.8 near-perfect.

Every point is computed in closed form as an exact expectation over uniform k-subsets, not simulated by drawing. Both curves are the mean over the four models of the per-target curve, bootstrapped after that averaging rather than by averaging the four published intervals: 20,000 resamples over targets, the same shared draw as every other interval on the page.

The bands are marginal intervals on the level, and they are wide because targets differ enormously. The claim does not rest on them. Going 32 to 512 is a paired quantity, and its interval is far tighter: delivered –, best in the pool –.

This figure uses the 160 targets every model folded, so the four contribute at every target. The rest of the page uses each model's own 160 or 161, which moves a per-model curve by at most –.

Method, limitations, the claims we withdrew, and how to reproduce every number:

What this is

Four independently trained predictors — Boltz-2, OpenDDE-abag and Protenix-v2, which are AF3-style all-atom diffusion co-folders, plus ESMFold2, a single-sequence folder — each sampled to 512 structures per target on the AbAg-XM panel, every structure scored with DockQ against the experimental reference. – labelled samples, folded on Tenstorrent Wormhole Galaxy hardware.

They are not four views of the same model, and they do not fail on the same targets: the pairwise overlap of their failure sets is – (Jaccard), and – are failed by all four. That partial independence is what the compute-split section buys.

Curves are exact, not simulated: every point is computed in closed form rather than drawn. Intervals are a 20,000-resample paired bootstrap over targets.

Prior work

That deep sampling raises the ceiling faster than confidence ranking delivers is already published for antibody–antigen complexes and is not claimed as new here. Zhu et al. (Bioinformatics 2026) report best-of-N mean DockQ rising from below 0.3 to above 0.5 by 200 samples across 110 targets, and name identifying the best generated model as the remaining bottleneck. OpenDDE's own technical report measures the same ranked-versus-oracle gap at its default N = 5.

What this panel adds is that gap expressed as an effective N, the split of ranking failure into within-target and across-target, the finding that most failures are epitope-level rather than pose-level, the price of model diversity against sampling depth at measured compute, and a pre-declared null on fixing selection with the scores a model already returns.

Limitations, denominators and provenance
  • The panel is complete: – model–target cells. Every cell is – samples deep, by construction — no cell is shallower, so the headline N is not an average over a ragged panel. Two things keep a cell out of the analysis and neither is missing data. Three of the 164 targets (9ly2, 9ly3, 9lz2) are three-way antibody–antigen hetero-hexamers whose interface the DockQ scorer does not resolve, in any model, so they carry no labels at all. And one cell is dropped as a mis-fold: OpenDDE-abag on 9sbb folds into a pTM 0.67–0.70 basin on the Galaxy against 0.91 for the same input refolded on Blackhole (DockQ 0.02 against 0.88 under the same scorer); a scan of 41 paired targets found it the only such case. Per model: –
  • Code provenance, and we would rather you heard it from us. No cell in this panel was folded by tt-bio as it stands on main today; the campaign deliberately ran one frozen engine tree so every cell is comparable. – cells — on the four largest targets, which needed memory fixes that were not in that tree — were folded on two later trees, each cell entirely on one tree. Those fixes are bit-exact at their own gates, but the trees are not byte-identical and we do not claim they are. Every headline is therefore computed with those cells included and excluded: the largest movement in any of them is –, and –. One concession: the exact commit of the later tree is not recoverable, because its stamp was inherited when the tree was copied.
  • The oracle is an upper bound, not an achievable number. "Best in the pool" is computed with the answer key, which is what oracle-best-of-N means throughout this literature. It cannot leak into the delivered number: the two curves share only the weighting, differ only in what they sort by, and the one tie-break that touches both resolves against the selector, so every delivered figure here is the conservative one.
  • Pooling checks. Truncating each 512-sample pool back to its first 256 reproduces our published N=256 statistics exactly, on 635 of the 643 analysed cells (the other 8 have no complete 256 rung to nest against, so they are unchecked rather than failed). Every analysed cell holds exactly – rows, and repeated structures within a pool: –.
  • Seeds are per model and per chunk, so they are shared across targets. Independence across targets comes from the inputs differing, not the draws differing — the diffusion noise tensor is target-shaped, so no two targets receive the same noise. The four models' seed blocks are pairwise disjoint.
  • Epitope and CDR claims are quoted at their own depth, never at 512. Those labels are partial, badly so for ESMFold2's CDR-H3. Mean samples labelled per target: –. Doubling the sample count did not double these.
  • One selector is stored on a coarse grid — – — so ties are common, and our tie-break credits the selector with the worse of two samples it cannot separate. That is the direction that flatters the headline, so we measured it: resolving ties the other way moves its delivered DockQ by –, far inside the interval.
  • Cost basis. – analysed samples carry no measured wall time, because their fleet record landed under the wrong rung label. The cost model takes a per-target median, so three targets' cost rests on seven of their eight chunks.
  • Four specific models at specific settings. ESMFold2 runs single-sequence; OpenDDE-abag is antibody-specialised, so its lead is not a general co-folding result.
  • This measures each model's own shipped confidence, not published rankers. The PAE-based scores (pDockQ2, ipSAE, and AntiConf, which builds on pDockQ2) cannot be computed here at all — PAE matrices were not written during the campaign. Learned rankers that score structures directly (DeepRank-Ab, ABAG-Rank) could have been run and were not. Whether a learned ranker closes the gap is untested here.
What changed from our first release

That release ran to 256 samples per target. This one runs to 512 on a complete panel, and several things we said then do not survive it. We would rather delete a claim than quietly keep it.

  • "Effective N is 1 to 3 samples" is no longer the headline. It was one reading of a flatter fact: effective N does not grow with N at all, so selection efficiency falls as 1/N. The number is still there, now as one point on a law.
  • "No knee at 512" is withdrawn. The gap's second difference is negative with an interval excluding zero in two of the four models. The ceiling itself still shows no saturation.
  • "Delivered quality declines with N for OpenDDE-abag" is withdrawn as a headline. We registered that test in advance at k = 8 and it came back −0.0072 [−0.0148, +0.0006], crossing zero. It is reported in the findings document with its interval. The intervals at k = 16 and k = 32 do exclude zero, and we are deliberately not promoting them — moving to whichever k clears the bar is the exact error the pre-registration exists to prevent. What replaced it is stronger: the tail inversion above, which is measured against its own control.
  • The cost-to-80% fit ran over 1..256 and now runs over 1..512, and reports how much the answer depends on where the data stops.
  • The exclusion lists are gone. The four targets we could not fold for device-memory reasons, and the five whose pools were short, are all complete now. So is the data cut-off.
  • One correction to the panel itself. Our completeness check counted label files rather than labels, and one cell of 656 carried a full set of scorer-error placeholders behind a well-formed file. It is fixed, rescored and included, and the check now counts populated values.
Reproduce
  • Models — Boltz-2, OpenDDE-abag, Protenix-v2, ESMFold2 as shipped in tt-bio at commit 6d352084c, stock checkpoints, inference only.
  • Targets — the AbAg-XM panel, 164 antibody–antigen complexes, 161 of them scorable, each with an experimentally solved reference.
  • Depth and seeds — 512 samples per target per model, run as eight 64-sample chunks. Base seed 40000 Boltz-2, 20000 OpenDDE-abag, 30000 Protenix-v2, 50000 ESMFold2; chunk j uses base + 1000·j, so the shallower rungs are exact prefixes of the deeper ones and the 512-sample pool is a strict superset of every rung below it.
  • Settings — the three co-folders fold with MSAs served from a precomputed cache (--msa_cache_only --max_parallel_samples 5, Boltz-2 8); ESMFold2 is single-sequence (--single_sequence --recycling_steps 10 --sampling_steps 100); --host_threads 8 throughout. Everything else as shipped by each model.
  • Hardware and cost — one 32-chip Tenstorrent Wormhole Galaxy. Card-hours are measured, not modelled: – per sample.
  • Metrics — DockQ against the reference, 0 to 1. Best is the highest-DockQ structure in the pool, what a perfect chooser would return; delivered is the one the model's own selector ranks first (confidence_score, mean pLDDT for ESMFold2). Epitope overlap is the Jaccard index of predicted against true antigen contact residues. Effective N is where the best-of-k curve crosses the delivered level.
  • Run it — python3 analysis/build_insights.py -o data/insights.json rebuilds every number on this page from the per-sample table (one row per structure with its DockQ, confidence and epitope labels). Publishing that table is a separate decision; the aggregates this page reads are committed in data/insights.json.

Full statistics, every interval and every control are in the findings document.
