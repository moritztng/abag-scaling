How this was measured

What this is

Four independently trained predictors, each sampled to 512 structures per target on the AbAg-XM panel, every structure scored with DockQ against the experimental reference. Boltz-2, OpenDDE-abag and Protenix-v2 are AF3-style all-atom diffusion co-folders; ESMFold2 is a single-sequence folder. – labelled samples, folded on Tenstorrent Wormhole Galaxy hardware.

They do not fail on the same targets: the pairwise overlap of their failure sets is – (Jaccard), and – are failed by all four. That partial independence is what the compute-split section buys.

Prior work

That deep sampling raises the ceiling faster than confidence ranking delivers is already published for antibody–antigen complexes, not new here. Fromm et al. (Bioinformatics 2026) report best-of-N mean DockQ rising from below 0.3 to above 0.5 by 200 samples across 110 targets, and name identifying the best generated model as the remaining bottleneck. OpenDDE's technical report measures the same ranked-versus-oracle gap at its default N = 5.

This panel adds that gap expressed as an effective N, the split of ranking failure into within-target and across-target, the finding that most failures are epitope-level rather than pose-level, the price of model diversity against sampling depth at measured compute, and a pre-declared null on fixing selection with the scores a model already returns.

Limitations, denominators and provenance

  • The panel is complete: – model–target cells, every one – samples deep by construction, so the headline N is not an average over a ragged panel. Two exclusions, neither of them missing data. Three of the 164 targets (9ly2, 9ly3, 9lz2) are three-way antibody–antigen hetero-hexamers whose interface the DockQ scorer does not resolve in any model, so they carry no labels. One cell is dropped as a mis-fold: OpenDDE-abag on 9sbb folds into a pTM 0.67–0.70 basin on the Galaxy against 0.91 for the same input refolded on Blackhole (DockQ 0.02 against 0.88 under the same scorer), the only such case in a scan of 41 paired targets. Per model: –
  • Code provenance. No cell was folded by tt-bio as it stands on main today; the campaign ran one frozen engine tree so every cell is comparable. – cells, on the four largest targets, needed memory fixes absent from that tree and ran on two later trees, each cell entirely on one. Those fixes are bit-exact at their own gates, but the trees are not byte-identical and we do not claim they are. Every headline is computed with those cells in and out: the largest movement is –, and –. The later tree's exact commit is not recoverable; its stamp was inherited when the tree was copied.
  • The oracle is an upper bound, not an achievable number. It cannot leak into the delivered number: the two curves share only the weighting, differ only in what they sort by, and the one tie-break that touches both resolves against the selector, so every delivered figure here is the conservative one.
  • Pooling checks. Truncating each 512-sample pool to its first 256 reproduces our published N=256 statistics exactly on 635 of the 643 analysed cells; the other 8 have no complete 256 rung to nest against, so they are unchecked rather than failed. Every analysed cell holds exactly – rows, and repeated structures within a pool: –.
  • Seeds are per model and per chunk, so they are shared across targets. Independence comes from the inputs differing, not the draws: the noise tensor is target-shaped, so no two targets receive the same noise. The four models' seed blocks are pairwise disjoint.
  • Epitope and CDR claims are quoted at their own depth, never at 512. Those labels are partial, badly so for ESMFold2's CDR-H3. Mean samples labelled per target: –. Doubling the sample count did not double these.
  • One selector is stored on a coarse grid — – — so ties are common, and our tie-break credits the selector with the worse of two samples it cannot separate. That is the direction that flatters the headline, so we measured it: resolving ties the other way moves its delivered DockQ by –, far inside the interval.
  • Cost basis. – analysed samples carry no measured wall time: their fleet record landed under the wrong rung label. The cost model takes a per-target median, so three targets' cost rests on seven of their eight chunks.
  • Four specific models at specific settings. ESMFold2 runs single-sequence; OpenDDE-abag is antibody-specialised, so its lead is not a general co-folding result.
  • This measures each model's own shipped confidence, not published rankers. The PAE-based scores (pDockQ2, ipSAE, and AntiConf, which builds on pDockQ2) cannot be computed here at all: PAE matrices were not written during the campaign. Learned rankers scoring structures directly (DeepRank-Ab, ABAG-Rank) could have been run and were not, so whether one closes the gap is untested.

What changed from our first release

That release ran to 256 samples per target. This one runs to 512 on a complete panel.

  • "Effective N is 1 to 3 samples" is no longer the headline, only one reading of a flatter fact: effective N does not grow with N at all, so selection efficiency falls as 1/N. The number is still there, as one point on a law.
  • "No knee at 512" is withdrawn. The gap's second difference is negative with an interval excluding zero in two of the four models. The ceiling itself still shows no saturation.
  • "Delivered quality declines with N for OpenDDE-abag" is withdrawn as a headline. We pre-registered that test at k = 8 and it came back −0.0072 [−0.0148, +0.0006], crossing zero; the findings document reports it with its interval. The intervals at k = 16 and k = 32 do exclude zero and we are deliberately not promoting them: moving to whichever k clears the bar is the exact error the pre-registration exists to prevent. The tail inversion above replaces it, measured against its own control.
  • The cost-to-80% fit ran over 1..256 and now runs over 1..512, and reports how much the answer depends on where the data stops.
  • The exclusion lists are gone. The four targets we could not fold for device-memory reasons, and the five whose pools were short, are all complete now. So is the data cut-off.
  • One correction to the panel itself. Our completeness check counted label files rather than labels, and one cell of 656 carried a full set of scorer-error placeholders behind a well-formed file. It is fixed, rescored and included; the check now counts populated values.

Reproduce

  • Models — Boltz-2, OpenDDE-abag, Protenix-v2, ESMFold2 as shipped in tt-bio at commit 6d352084c, stock checkpoints, inference only.
  • Targets — the AbAg-XM panel, 164 antibody–antigen complexes, 161 of them scorable, each with an experimentally solved reference.
  • Depth and seeds — 512 samples per target per model, run as eight 64-sample chunks. Base seed 40000 Boltz-2, 20000 OpenDDE-abag, 30000 Protenix-v2, 50000 ESMFold2; chunk j uses base + 1000·j, so the shallower rungs are exact prefixes of the deeper ones: the 512-sample pool is a strict superset of every rung below it.
  • Settings — the three co-folders fold with MSAs served from a precomputed cache (--msa_cache_only --max_parallel_samples 5, Boltz-2 8); ESMFold2 is single-sequence (--single_sequence --recycling_steps 10 --sampling_steps 100); --host_threads 8 throughout. Everything else as shipped by each model.
  • Hardware and cost — one 32-chip Tenstorrent Wormhole Galaxy. Card-hours are measured, not modelled: – per sample.
  • Metrics — DockQ against the reference, 0 to 1. Epitope overlap is the Jaccard index of predicted against true antigen contact residues. Effective N is where the best-of-k curve crosses the delivered level.
  • Run it — python3 analysis/build_insights.py -o data/insights.json rebuilds every number on this page from the per-sample table, one row per structure with its DockQ, confidence and epitope labels. Publishing that table is a separate decision; the aggregates this page reads are committed in data/insights.json.

Full statistics, every interval and every control are in the findings document.
